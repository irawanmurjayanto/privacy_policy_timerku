TimeKu Privacy Policy Last updated: October 30, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how the TimeKu application (“App”) handles information when you use its features. By using TimeKu, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. 1. Information We Process Depending on the features you use, TimeKu may process the following information: App settings: sound preferences, timer settings, text-to-speech (TTS) messages, selected skins, and other preferences saved so they remain available when you reopen the App. Activity history: activity labels, notes, durations, start and completion times, and timer status that you enter. Files you select: MP3 files used for alarm sounds, and photos or videos you attach to activity records, if you choose to use those features. Technical and advertising information: third-party advertising services may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, and ad interactions, subject to the provider's policies and your privacy settings. 2. How Information Is Used Information may be used to operate the timer, TTS announcements, and alarms; save and display activity history and attachments; remember your app preferences; maintain and improve the App's functionality and stability; and display advertisements through third-party advertising services where available. 3. Local Storage and Google Drive App settings, history, and certain files may be stored locally on your device. If you choose to use a backup feature or Google Drive integration, the data you select may be transferred to your Google Drive account. Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for the Google account you use and can manage or delete backup files through Google Drive. Backup availability depends on the version of the App and the features enabled. 4. Third-Party Services TimeKu may use third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements and Google Drive when you choose to use its integration. Locally stored data may be lost if you uninstall the App, clear its data, or experience device failure, unless you have made a backup. 8. Children's Privacy TimeKu is not specifically designed for children.
